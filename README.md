## 📌 About the Project

How can retail sales data help businesses understand their performance and make better decisions?

This project explores that question through an interactive **Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard**, developed using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard transforms retail data into meaningful visual insights, helping analyze sales performance, product categories, outlet characteristics, and location-based trends.

The goal is to move beyond raw numbers and present business information in a clear, interactive, and decision-oriented format.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- 📊 Evaluate overall sales performance using key metrics.
- 🛍️ Identify product categories contributing to sales.
- 🏪 Compare sales performance across different outlet types.
- 📍 Analyze sales across different outlet location tiers.
- 📈 Explore outlet establishment trends and their relationship with sales.
- 🔎 Build an interactive dashboard for business reporting and exploration.

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard focuses on four major business metrics:

| KPI | Purpose |
|:---|:---|
| 💰 Total Sales | Measures overall sales revenue. |
| 📦 Number of Items | Indicates the quantity of items represented in the dataset. |
| 🧾 Average Sales | Helps evaluate average sales performance. |
| ⭐ Average Rating | Summarizes customer ratings. |

---

## 🔍 Analysis & Dashboard Features

### 1. 🛍️ Product Performance Analysis
Explore sales across different item categories and understand how product types contribute to overall performance.

### 2. 🏪 Outlet Type Analysis
Compare different outlet types to understand variations in sales and business performance.

### 3. 📍 Location-Based Analysis
Examine sales across outlet location tiers and explore differences in regional performance.

### 4. 📏 Outlet Size Analysis
Investigate the relationship between outlet size and sales performance.

### 5. 📅 Outlet Establishment Analysis
Explore sales patterns based on outlet establishment year.

### 6. 🥑 Fat Content Analysis
Analyze sales distribution across different fat-content categories.
